Betting ledgerPaper

Locked before puck drop, graded in public. Every moneyline call is timestamped before the game and never edited; every prop bet is priced against the book at lock and scored against the closing line. No real money, no picks sold -- this is the model keeping score on itself.

Bankroll curve

Cumulative profit as a share of the 10,000-unit paper bankroll, bet by bet, at the price taken.

Closing-line value

Each prop bet's CLV (close minus lock, probability points on our side) and the running average. Above zero = the market moved our way.

Moneyline log-loss, running

Calibration

Moneyline calls grouped by the favorite's locked probability: how often that favorite won. On the diagonal = honest probabilities.

Why the misses happened

Every graded call, classified the next morning from the game's expected goals and the goalies and lineups that actually played.

By market

Each market is judged on its own at 100 scored bets (pre-registered); until then the read is too small to call.
